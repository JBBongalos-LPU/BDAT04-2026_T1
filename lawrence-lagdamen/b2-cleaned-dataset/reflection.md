The reason being those duplicate SaleIDs being removed is because sales data should uniquely represent one sale, and keeping the extra records could overstate sales activity. Furthermore, this activity also taught me that not every unusual value should be rendered irrelevant. In the case of the CustomerIDs, upon further inspection, a total of 4,775 blank valued data or 45% of sales was discovered. However, this does not necessarily represent a data-quality defect rather it represents the company’s total walk-in customers and removing such data would mean removing valid transactions that would significantly distort the company’s sales data.

However, the number of missing CustomerIDs striked my interest because of how many they are.
