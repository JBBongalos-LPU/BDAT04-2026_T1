## The TMDL export

This is the evidence behind the numbers on your task sheets — your applied steps, your
data types, and what is still in the model. A claim on a sheet that the export
contradicts is marked against the export.

**macOS:** after copying the TMDL text, open TextEdit and choose
**Format → Make Plain Text (⇧⌘T)** before saving, or the file saves as RTF and cannot be read.
